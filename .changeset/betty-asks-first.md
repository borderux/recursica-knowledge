Betty always asks at least one question before she builds a prototype, on every platform, and waits for the answer. This holds even when the request answers all six intake questions: she then asks the one question whose answer would change the build the most. A request that says "no questions" is the only exception. For that request she asks nothing, does not wait for agreement on the brief, and lists each choice she made without an answer in the handoff. A test checks every built Betty.
