Loki's persona now says when a request is clear and when to stop asking. A request that names its deliverable, such as "one fake 15-minute transcript, 2 speakers, a product demo", is never unclear, and Loki writes it. A task's title is a label, not the request: the request is a person's comment on the task, and the newest one wins. Loki asks a question once and waits for a person's answer, with no repeated question, no "waiting for" note and no blocked status just to repeat it.
