Loki on RecursicaChat 2.0 says interviews can be in English or Spanish. His RecursicaChat instructions get a short "Languages" passage: the request or a persona file's "Interview language" line chooses the language, one set can mix the two, English is the default, and other languages are not supported yet. When asked what he can do, he says so. A new second starter, "One 15-minute interview in [English or Spanish] about [your subject]", opens the fill-in dialog with a field for each. Loki 0.4.0.
